Frequently Asked Questions

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

It depends on your home, pets, allergies and recent renovations. Many homeowners consider cleaning every few years, or sooner if they notice heavy dust, odors or visible debris in vents.


Why does a dryer vent need cleaning?

Lint builds up in the vent over time. It restricts airflow, makes your dryer work harder and is a common cause of dryer fires, so regular cleaning is important.
